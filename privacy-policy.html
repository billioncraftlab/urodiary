Privacy Policy

App: UroDiary: Urine Tracker  |  Developer: BillionCraft Lab  |  Effective Date: November 26, 2026

1. Overview

BillionCraft Lab ("we", "our", or "us") built UroDiary: Urine Tracker as a personal hydration, fluid intake, and voiding habit tracking application. We respect your privacy and designed the app to work offline-first with user-controlled data storage and transparent privacy controls.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All fluid intake logs, voiding logs, timestamps, container presets, profile details, reminders, and app preferences you enter in UroDiary are stored locally on your Android device in encrypted local storage. We do not operate external backend servers that collect, view, or store your personal health or habit logs.

3. Anonymous Telemetry, Crash Reports, and Analytics

To improve app stability, diagnose technical issues, and understand general feature usage, UroDiary may collect anonymous diagnostic and usage information associated solely with a randomly generated, non-personally identifiable anonymous user ID (anonymousUserId). This anonymous identifier is never linked to your name, email address, Google account, or personal fluid/voiding logs.

  • Analytics: Collects aggregated, non-personal app usage events and screen interaction metrics to help improve usability.
  • Crash Report & Diagnostics: Collects anonymous crash logs, stack traces, device diagnostics, and other app performance data to identify and fix bugs.
  • User Control: Both Analytics and Crash Report are enabled by default, and you can turn either option off at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy and Data.

4. Advertising and Personalized Ads

The free version of UroDiary displays advertisements (such as banner ads) provided by third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob). Users who purchase the optional one-time UroDiary Plus — Lifetime upgrade enjoy an ad-free experience.

  • Personalized Ads Control: When ads are active, advertising SDKs may process device or advertising identifiers and ad interaction data in accordance with Google's advertising policies.
  • User Choice: Personalized Ads is enabled by default, and you can disable personalized advertising at any time under Settings > Privacy and Data to switch to non-personalized ads (or upgrade to UroDiary Plus to remove ads completely).

5. Local Exports and File Sharing

If you choose to export your logs or generate backup files (such as CSV, PDF, or JSON/ZIP backup files) to save on your device or share with a healthcare provider or third party, those files are created and transmitted solely through your explicit action using your device's standard share or file picker tools. BillionCraft Lab never automatically uploads or accesses your exported files.

6. Optional Google Drive Backup

If you choose to sign in with your Google Account and use the optional Google Drive Backup feature, UroDiary accesses only the dedicated hidden application folder (drive.appdata scope) in your personal Google Drive to create, restore, or delete your backup file. We do not access any other files in your Google Drive, nor do we transfer or sell your backup data to third parties.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your local diary entries and preferences remain on your device until you use Clear Data inside the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall UroDiary. If you created a Google Drive backup, you can permanently delete it at any time using the Delete Backup option inside the app or by revoking UroDiary's access in your Google Account security settings. For privacy inquiries, contact us at billioncraftlab.dev@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

UroDiary is intended for general adult audiences (18 and over) and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates, new features, or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact BillionCraft Lab at billioncraftlab.dev@gmail.com.
