UroDiary: Urine Tracker — User Guide

App: UroDiary: Urine Tracker (v1.0.0+1)  |  Developer: BillionCraft Lab  |  Updated: November 26, 2026

UroDiary is an offline-first mobile application for tracking fluid intake, urine output, and daily habits with zero sign-up screens or external servers. All records are stored locally on your device in encrypted storage unless you explicitly export a report or run an encrypted backup.

1. Top Bar, Navigation & Profile Switching

  1. 4-Tab Bottom Navigation: Switch anytime between Log, Records, Trends, and Doctor. The bottom navigation bar stays accessible across the main tabs and throughout Settings.
  2. Settings Access: Tap the Settings gear icon on the top-left of the app bar to open preferences, profile details, reminders, and backup tools.
  3. Quick Profile Switcher: Tap the profile pill on the top-right (showing Self or Spouse, or your saved first name once customized) to switch active profiles immediately.

2. Logging Entries (Log Tab)

The Log tab displays live Voided and Intake daily totals at the top for your active clinical tracking day (calculated using your configured Day Start Time and displayed in mL or oz).

Step-by-Step Logging

  1. Select Entry Type: Toggle between Void and Intake inside the main card.
  2. Set Date & Time: The date/time box tracks the current device date and time automatically (in 12h or 24h format), and shows Last void or Last intake above it (with an (i) info icon). Tap the date/time box to open the 3D cylindrical wheel selector (Date, Hour, Minute, and AM/PM in 12-hour mode), then tap Today / Now to sync or Done to confirm.
  3. Adjust Volume: Use the − and + steppers (which step by your configured output or intake increment), tap the volume field directly to type a value (1..3000 mL or equivalent oz), or tap a quick preset chip (your default container preset, two secondary presets, or Custom).
  4. Add Void Details (When in Void Mode):
    • Toggle Unmeasured Void (Log bathroom visit without volume) if you are recording a visit without a measured volume—this sets volume to 0 mL, disables volume controls/presets.
    • Expand Optional Details (More / Less) to choose Urgency (None, Mild, Moderate, Urgent), pick one of 6 Urine Color swatches (Clear, Pale, Straw, Yellow, Dark, Brown), and select symptoms/conditions (After sleep, Foamy urine, Leakage, plus Catheter if enabled in Settings).
  5. Add Intake Details (When in Intake Mode): Choose the Beverage (Water, Coffee, Tea, Juice, Alcohol, Other) and set Bladder Impact (Smooth or Bladder irritant; Coffee and Alcohol automatically suggest Bladder irritant).
  6. Add a Note & Save: Tap + Add Note to type a note and tap Done, then tap Save Log at the bottom to save the entry locally.

3. Viewing & Managing History (Records Tab)

  1. Browse by Date: Use the 7-day strip (aligned to your chosen Week Start Day, Sunday or Monday, showing per-day In and Out totals) or tap Full Month to open the floating month calendar overlay.
  2. Check Daily Balance: Review your target or limit progress bar alongside Water In, Urine Out, and In - Out.
  3. Filter, Edit, or Delete Logs: Filter the day's list using All, Void, or Intake. Tap the edit (pencil) icon on an entry to update its volume or timestamp, or tap the delete (trash) icon to remove it after confirmation.
  4. Clear a Day or Date Range: Tap Clear / Range to open Manage Saved Records, where you can choose Clear Selected Day or Delete Date Range (using a week-aware date picker aligned to your clinical day boundary).

4. Tracking Trends & Adherence (Trends Tab)

  1. Choose a Timeframe: Select 7 Days, 14 Days (default), 30 Days, or Custom (pick From Date and To Date and tap Apply).
  2. Review Adherence & Paired Chart: Check the adherence banner for compliant clinical days against your target or limit, and tap columns on the In vs. Out capsule bar chart to inspect period averages.
  3. Read Clinical Summary Metrics: Scroll through the 9 summary metrics: Avg Daily Water Intake, Avg Daily Urine Out, Peak Void Volume, After-Sleep Void, Avg Interval, Avg after sleep void, Total unmeasured voids recorded, Avg Urgency reported, and Avg Leakage reported.

5. Preparing & Exporting Doctor Reports (Doctor Tab)

  1. Select Reporting Window: Choose 24hour, 7 Days (default), 30Days, or Custom.
  2. Update Patient Details & Visit Note: Review your profile name, DOB, and auto-calculated age, and enter any Medications / Visit Note.
  3. Copy or Export: Tap Copy Plain Text Summary to copy a clinical overview (including Urgency & Leaks, Foamy Urine, Catheter Void, and Unmeasured Void), or use PDF Export, Share PDF, or CSV Export to generate a report.

6. Settings, Reminders, Backups & Plus

  • Profiles & Daily Targets: Switch active profiles (Self / Spouse), edit First Name, Last Name, and DOB under Profile Details, and configure Limit (Heart/Kidney) or Target (Hydration) under Daily Fluid Target / Limit.
  • Containers & Formats: Customize container names, default volumes, and +/− steps in Default Containers & Step. In Default Formats, set Volume Unit (mL or oz), Time Format (12h or 24h), Day Start Time (default 00:00), Week Start Day (Sunday or Monday), and Log Display Order (Old to New (Ascending) or New to Old (Descending)).
  • Reminders & Display: Configure Stop Drinking After (off by default; default 20:00) with the 3D wheel and Send Test Notification, toggle Display Catheter on Log, and switch Dark Mode on or off.
  • Local & Google Drive Backup: Use Local Drive Backup to export or restore an encrypted .json backup file, or open Google Drive Backup to sign in with your Google account and manage backups (Create Backup, Restore Backup, Delete Backup) in UroDiary's private Google Drive app-data folder (drive.appdata). You can also use Clear Data to wipe the active profile's logs after confirmation.
  • Privacy, App Info & Plus: Configure Analytics, Crash Report, and Personalized Ads (all enabled by default and linked to an anonymous user ID) under Settings > Privacy and Data, open Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, or User Guide under Settings > App Information, upgrade via UroDiary Plus — Lifetime, or use Review on Play Store and Share with Your Friends.
